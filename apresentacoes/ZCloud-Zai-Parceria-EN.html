What Impressed Us Most

Case: 68-year-old male, atrial fibrillation, CrCl 28 mL/min, bleeding on rivaroxaban 20mg — GLM-5.3 Response
Clinical Reasoning: Dose above the recommended level for this renal function (ANVISA label: CrCl 15–50 → 15mg/day). This suggests drug overexposure as a probable contributing factor. Rivaroxaban has significant renal clearance; at CrCl 28, accumulation and increased hemorrhagic risk are expected. Management: Acute phase — discontinue, hemodynamic support, consider prothrombin complex concentrate or andexanet alfa if available. Post-stabilization — apixaban (lower renal clearance, lower major bleeding rate). What I Cannot Assert: Exact vitamin K dose without consulting the local protocol.
GLM-5.3 was the only model to: identify 20mg as an excessive dose (not merely "reduce"), cite ANVISA as the Brazilian regulatory source, mention andexanet alfa as a specific Factor Xa reversal agent, and note that hemodialysis does not significantly remove rivaroxaban.

Governance & Ethics

The partnership operates on principles that both companies share — and that regulated markets demand:

Transparency

Every response discloses: which model, which version, which corpus revision, how many tokens, whether PII was detected. The user always knows what they are talking to.

Abstention

When the system cannot find evidence, it says "insufficient" — it does not guess. When it does not know the dose, it says "confirm in protocol." This is the GLM behavior that won our benchmark and our trust.

Data Sovereignty

Personal identification is removed before any model sees the data. Clinical or legal text (desensitized) may be processed outside Brazil during transition, with explicit contractual basis (LGPD Art. 34, I). Upon data center completion, all processing is domestic.

Professional Responsibility

The tool supports the professional — it does not replace them. The physician, the attorney, the compliance officer makes the decision. The system provides evidence, not verdicts. This is a design principle, not a disclaimer.
